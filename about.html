The Instrument Journey

Surgical instruments go through several steps after they leave the operating room. This page gives a basic overview of that process.

1. After the Surgical Case

Used instruments leave the operating room and are sent to Sterile Processing for reprocessing.

2. Decontamination

Instruments are cleaned according to the requirements for the device before they move to the clean side of the department.

3. Inspection and Assembly

The instruments are inspected for cleanliness, condition, and function. They are then placed back into their surgical sets.

4. Sterilization

The completed sets are prepared and processed using the correct sterilization method for the instruments inside them.

5. Storage and Return to the OR

After processing, the sets are stored or prepared for upcoming cases until they are needed in the operating room again.

Why I Chose This Topic

I worked as a surgical technologist for about two and a half years before moving into Sterile Processing. Working in both areas gave me a better understanding of how much the OR and SPD depend on each other.
