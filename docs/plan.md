# Project Plan

**Project Name:** SPD-to-OR  
**Project Manager:** Patrick Casey

## Risk Analysis

For each risk, I rated the impact and probability from 1 to 5. I multiplied those numbers to get the risk score. Risks with a score of 12 or higher need a plan.

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Score | TAME Strategy | Plan |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Running short on time because of work, school, or family | 5 | 4 | 20 | Mitigate | Finish the required parts first and avoid adding extra features. |
| GitHub Pages or links not working | 5 | 3 | 15 | Mitigate | Test the links before submitting and check the live website. |
| Getting confused about which file is the newest | 4 | 3 | 12 | Mitigate | Keep everything in one project folder and use GitHub commits instead of making several copies. |
| Adding too much to the project | 4 | 3 | 12 | Eliminate | Stick to the two required pages and required documentation. |
| Website information being too technical | 3 | 3 | 9 | Accept | Keep the wording simple and explain SPD for readers who may not know what it is. |

## TAME

- **Transfer:** I do not have a major risk that needs to be transferred because this is an individual project.
- **Accept:** I can accept small design issues if the website still works and meets the requirements.
- **Mitigate:** I can lower the chance of time, publishing, and file problems by testing and checking my work as I go.
- **Eliminate:** I can eliminate scope creep by not adding features that are outside the assignment.
