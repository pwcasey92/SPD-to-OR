What is Sterile Processing?

Sterile Processing, often called SPD, is the department that cleans, inspects, prepares, sterilizes, and stores surgical instruments so they can be used again safely.

SPD works behind the scenes, but it has a direct connection to what happens in the operating room. The OR depends on having the right instruments ready when they are needed.

What SPD Does

  • Receives used surgical instruments
  • Cleans and inspects instruments
  • Assembles surgical instrument sets
  • Prepares items for sterilization
  • Stores or distributes completed sets

Why the SPD and OR Relationship Matters

Communication between Sterile Processing and the operating room can help prevent delays and make it easier to solve problems with instruments or equipment before they affect a surgical case.
